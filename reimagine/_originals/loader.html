Loader concepts

each plays in a mini screen with the hero behind it · press Replay · all about 1.5–2 s

01 — signature

Your name signs itself in the hero's handwriting (red P and S), a red line draws under it, then the screen wipes up to reveal the site.

02 — counter curtain

A huge 0→100 counter bottom-left with a red line, small words cycling (Designing · Building · Shipping); at 100 the whole panel lifts like a curtain.

03 — pixel dissolve

Retro: the skull bobs over a pixel progress bar, then the screen breaks into pixels that vanish randomly (matches your pixel background).

04 — terminal boot

Quick boot log (loading projects… ok, fetching activity… ok), then it blurs away. Very developer.

05 — split words

Words flash fast (Design. Build. Ship.) then your name; the screen splits along a red line and the halves slide apart.

06 — logo ring

Your skull logo breathing inside a red ring that fills with the percentage; the logo shrinks and the screen fades. Closest to now, but cleaner.
